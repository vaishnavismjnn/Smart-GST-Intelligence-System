## Intelligent Receipt Information Extraction System

## PROBLEM STATEMENT
Small businesses and freelancers manage many receipts and invoices for accounting and GST purposes. Most of this work is still done manually by storing documents as paper or images. Invoice details are manually entered into spreadsheets or billing software. This process takes a lot of time and can easily lead to human errors. It also becomes difficult to organize, track, and manage invoices efficiently.

The Smart GST Billing System is an all-in-one platform designed to simplify billing, expense tracking, and GST compliance for businesses. Users can upload receipts and invoices, and the system uses AI-powered OCR to automatically extract key details such as vendor name, amount, GST, and invoice number, eliminating manual data entry and reducing errors. It also enables quick generation of GST-compliant invoices with automatic calculations and downloadable PDFs, ensuring professional and consistent billing. All records are securely stored in a searchable digital archive, allowing easy access and organization. Additionally, the system provides real-time business insights like total sales, GST liability, and spending patterns, while generating GST reports for smooth and stress-free tax filing.
